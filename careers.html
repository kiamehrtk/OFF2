Careers

Come build live entertainment on the west coast.

We produce our own festivals, run our own rooms and handle production in house — which means the people here get their hands on the whole thing, not one slice of it.

The work

A season here runs from a warehouse in January to a valley at sunrise in June. Same crew, wildly different rooms — you learn fast because nothing repeats.

The team

Small enough that your call is the call. Programming, production and ticketing sit in the same room, so decisions don't disappear into a process.

The standard

We'd rather build one show properly than book four. If you care about the difference between good enough and actually right, you'll fit.

We hire in bursts around the season, and we keep good applications on file for when we do. If you work in production, lighting, sound, programming or front of house, introduce yourself — we'd rather know you before we need you.
