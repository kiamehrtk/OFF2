OFF2 Label

We sign records we'd play at our own shows.

OFF2 Label is the record arm of OFF2 Entertainment. Same crew, same rooms — demos go to the people who book the floors, not an inbox nobody opens.

The sound

House, techno, melodic and bass. If it works at Harbour at 1am it works here — we care whether a record moves a room, not which subgenre it belongs to.

The standard

Finished or near-finished, mixed, ideally mastered. We can hear past a rough master. We can't hear past a track that isn't built yet.

Before you send

No bootlegs, no uncleared samples, nothing already signed. One track is enough — send your best, not your catalogue.
